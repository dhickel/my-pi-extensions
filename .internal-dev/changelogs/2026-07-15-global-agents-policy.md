## Behavioral Impact

New Pi sessions now receive global instructions to use ready `.internal-dev` stores deliberately, preserve reusable corrected assumptions, complete accepted plans without stubs or speculative API growth, escalate blocking uncertainty through the installed senior-agent skill, prefer simple modern data-driven implementations, and validate work before claiming success.

## Risks

- The strict escalation trigger may invoke the senior-agent skill more often when routine inspection does not quickly establish a confident next action.
- The runtime policy applies only to new Pi sessions or sessions that reload context after installation.
